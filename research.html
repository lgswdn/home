Research

Currently, my research focus is mainly on contact-rich dexterous manipulation, from low-level control and planning to the connection with multimodal models and agentic methods.

I am also interested in combinatorial algorithms and data structures, and hope to explore how algorithmic ideas can contribute to more efficient and principled approaches in robotics (and other fields).
